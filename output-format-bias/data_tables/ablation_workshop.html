Across formats: binary vs. informed compression vs. descriptive prose

How to use this workshop. Every text element is editable: cell heading, cell body, badge text, student anchor (ID / name / pattern / expected), column header (name / subtitle / note), and table titles. Click into any text to edit; click out (or press Enter) to commit. A small dot appears in the top-left corner of cells you've edited. State autosaves to your browser under ofb-ablation-workshop-v3. Export per table or both combined as CSV. Reset returns everything to the defaults loaded at page load.

Cell encoding. Each cell shows an icon (○ correct CLEAR, ▲ flagged, □ missed TP, — not tested), a short bolder heading (e.g. 24/24 Clear — missed TP), and one to three sentences of context. The SELF-CONTRADICTING badge marks cells where the model's reasoning text contradicts its own flag. The DIAGNOSTIC red border marks the load-bearing S028/S029 swap evidence in the Test M column.
