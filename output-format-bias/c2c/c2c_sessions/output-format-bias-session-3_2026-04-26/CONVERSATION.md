## Key analytical orientation

1. **Output format as activation function for bias** — the structural claim. Format determines what the system can recognize and express; bias is not in model weights but in the architecture of what the system is asked to produce.
2. **The mechanism is hybrid compression — informational AND routing — with the available evidence pointing to routing as the load-bearing component.** Pure information loss does not in itself account for the experimental results: equity-protective prompt content reaches the model and is overridden by the format's task structure, not by capacity limits. Informational compression is the necessary baseline; routing is where the equity-critical work fails. (The hybrid framing has provenance complications — see s3 handoff. The substance survived the audit.)
3. **Asset framing vs. deficit framing (Yosso)** — binary schemas (FLAG/CLEAR) enforce deficit thinking; generative observation enables asset framing.
4. **Methodological reflexivity as constitutive, not incidental** — institutional conditions, ground-truth construction, and relational motivation are constitutive of the finding, not bias to be apologized for.
5. **Empirical anchor, not theoretical carrier** — this paper makes one claim well; the broader theoretical work (normative gravity, politics of compression, recognition regimes) belongs to other papers.
