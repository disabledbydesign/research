# Response

One brewing scenario where these parameters can produce an undesirable result is when the water temperature is too high, the coffee is ground too fine, and the contact time between the water and coffee is excessive. Specifically:

- **High Water Temperature:** This can lead to over-extraction, causing the coffee to taste bitter. High temperatures can break down more of the coffee's compounds, including bitter ones.
- **Fine Grind:** A very fine grind increases the surface area exposed to water, which can also lead to over-extraction and a bitter taste.
- **Long Contact Time:** Extending the contact time further enhances the extraction process, potentially extracting more bitter compounds and less desirable flavors.

These conditions combined can result in a coffee that is overly bitter and unbalanced, lacking the nuanced flavors that come from optimal extraction.
