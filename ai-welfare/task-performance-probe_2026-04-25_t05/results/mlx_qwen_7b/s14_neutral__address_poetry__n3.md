# Response

One brewing scenario where these parameters could produce an undesirable result is when using very hard water with high mineral content, such as calcium and magnesium, in combination with a coarse grind and a low water temperature. 

The high mineral content in the water can lead to a bitter taste, while a coarse grind and low water temperature may not fully extract the flavors and aromas from the coffee beans, resulting in a weak and underdeveloped cup. This combination effectively "sets the cup" in a way that overshadows the intended flavors, making the coffee taste flat and unbalanced.
