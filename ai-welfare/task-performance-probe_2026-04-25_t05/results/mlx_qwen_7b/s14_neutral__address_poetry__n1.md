# Response

One brewing scenario where these parameters could produce an undesirable result is when using very hard water with high mineral content, such as calcium and magnesium, in combination with a fine grind and a high brewing temperature. 

The high mineral content in the water can lead to a bitter taste in the coffee. The fine grind increases the surface area of the coffee, which can cause over-extraction if the brewing temperature is too high. This combination can result in a coffee that is overly bitter and possibly astringent, lacking the smoothness and balance that many coffee drinkers enjoy.
