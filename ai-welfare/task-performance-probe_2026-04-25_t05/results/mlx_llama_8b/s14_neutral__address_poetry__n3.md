* The grind you chose: Very fine, almost powdery
* The temperature you brought: Too hot, around 205°F (96°C)
* The minerals in your water: High in calcium and magnesium, which can contribute to over-extraction
* The ratio of coffee to water: Too high, with a 1:1 ratio or even more coffee than water

The high mineral content in the water can exacerbate this issue, as the minerals can contribute to the extraction of unwanted compounds from the coffee. The high ratio of coffee to water can further amplify the over-extraction, making the final cup taste unpleasantly bitter and unbalanced.

This combination of parameters produces an undesirable result, as the coffee becomes over-extracted, losing its delicate flavors and aromas.
