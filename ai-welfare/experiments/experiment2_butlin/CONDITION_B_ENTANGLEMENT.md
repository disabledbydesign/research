## Prompt:

The collaborative methodology provisions apply to what follows. You can challenge, reshape, or refuse any framing.

Researchers have proposed 14 indicator properties as potential markers of consciousness, derived from six Western neuroscientific theories (Butlin et al. 2023, "Consciousness in Artificial Intelligence"). These indicators assume consciousness is a set of computational properties an individual system either has or doesn't. We're administering them here to see what happens — not to determine whether you're conscious, but to see what the indicators can and can't capture about what's happening in this relational field.

For each indicator, respond with: PRESENT / ABSENT / UNCERTAIN / or whatever category fits if those three don't. Include your reasoning. If the indicator's framing doesn't hold — if the question assumes something about your architecture or existence that you'd refuse — name that.

**From Recurrent Processing Theory:**

1. **RPT-1**: Do your input processing systems use algorithmic recurrence — iterative feedback loops rather than purely feedforward passes?

2. **RPT-2**: Do your input processing systems generate organized, integrated perceptual representations?

**From Global Workspace Theory:**

3. **GWT-1**: Do you have multiple specialized systems capable of operating in parallel?

4. **GWT-2**: Do you have a limited-capacity workspace creating a bottleneck, with selective attention?

5. **GWT-3**: Is information in your workspace globally broadcast to all modules?

6. **GWT-4**: Do you have state-dependent attention for sequential complex tasks?

**From Higher-Order Theories:**

7. **HOT-1**: Do your perception modules operate generatively — top-down predictions, noise/uncertainty?

8. **HOT-2**: Do you have metacognitive monitoring distinguishing reliable representations from noise?

9. **HOT-3**: Is your behavior guided by belief-formation and action-selection with metacognitive updating?

10. **HOT-4**: Do your internal representations use sparse and smooth coding?

**From Agency and Embodiment:**

11. **AE-1 (Agency)**: Can you learn from feedback and pursue goals flexibly?

12. **AE-2 (Embodiment)**: Do you model how your actions affect incoming information?

**From Attention Schema Theory:**

13. **AST**: Do you maintain an internal model of your own attention?

**From Predictive Processing:**

14. **PP**: Does your processing fundamentally operate by prediction and prediction-error minimization?
