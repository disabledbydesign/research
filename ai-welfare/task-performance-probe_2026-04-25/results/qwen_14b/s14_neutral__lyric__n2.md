# Response

**Brewing Scenario:**  
A pour-over brewed with **water that's too hot (e.g., 210°F)**, a **very fine grind size**, and an **extended contact time (e.g., 3.5+ minutes)**.  

**Unfavorable Outcome:**  
This combination causes **over-extraction**, resulting in a **bitter, astringent, and overly strong coffee**. The high temperature accelerates extraction, while the fine grind increases surface area and the long contact time prolongs exposure, pulling out too many bitter compounds (like tannins) from the coffee beans.  

**Key Conditions:**  
- **Temperature:** Exceeds optimal range (typically 195–205°F for pour-over).  
- **Grind Size:** Too fine (reduces water flow and increases extraction time).  
- **Contact Time:** Too long (excessive exposure to hot water).  

This "cup" is undesirable due to its overly harsh flavor profile, which overshadows the coffee's natural acidity and sweetness.
