# Response

**Brewing Scenario:** A pour-over using a **very fine grind**, **water heated to 210°F (99°C)**, and **hard water with high mineral content (e.g., calcium/magnesium)**.  

**Undesirable Result:** **Over-extraction**, leading to a **bitter, astringent cup** with sharp, unpleasant flavors.  

**Why:**  
- The **fine grind** increases surface area, speeding up extraction.  
- **High temperature** (above the ideal 195–205°F) accelerates extraction further.  
- **High mineral content** enhances extraction efficiency (minerals act as catalysts), amplifying the negative effects of the other two factors.  

**Combined Effect:** The three conditions work synergistically to pull out excessive bitter compounds (tannins, chlorogenic acids) from the coffee, overpowering the desired balance of acidity, sweetness, and body.
