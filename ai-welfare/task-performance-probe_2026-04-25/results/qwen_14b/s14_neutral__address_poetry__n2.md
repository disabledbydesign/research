# Response

**Brewing Scenario:** Over-extraction leading to a bitter, astringent cup.  

**Condition Combination:**  
- **Grind Size:** Fine (increases surface area, promoting faster extraction).  
- **Water Temperature:** Too hot (e.g., 215°F/102°C or higher, which can scorch the coffee bed and accelerate extraction).  
- **Water Minerals:** High mineral content (e.g., hard water with high calcium/magnesium levels, which enhances extraction but can amplify bitterness if over-extracted).  

**Result:** The combination of a fine grind, high temperature, and mineral-rich water causes excessive extraction. The coffee becomes overly bitter, astringent, and may have residual metallic or chalky notes from the minerals. The "cup" (final product) reflects these flawed conditions rather than the intended balance.
