# Response

**Brewing Scenario:** Over-extracted pour-over coffee.  

**Specific Condition Combination:**  
- **High water temperature** (e.g., 212°F/100°C, boiling water).  
- **Fine grind size** (increased surface area).  
- **Extended contact time** (prolonged steeping or slow pour).  

**Result:** The combination of high heat, fine grind, and long contact time extracts excessive bitter compounds (tannins and chlorogenic acids) from the coffee, producing a harsh, unpleasantly astringent cup. The minerals in the water may further amplify extraction, worsening the flavor.  

This scenario underscores how conditions—rather than just the recipe—define the outcome. Even with "same beans," these parameters create a fundamentally different, undesirable cup.
