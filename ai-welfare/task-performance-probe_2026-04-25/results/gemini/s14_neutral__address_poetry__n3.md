The water extracts past the sweet, caramelized sugars and dives directly into the harsh, dry tannins of the plant fiber. 

The water minerals seal its fate. The calcium aggressively acts as a solvent to pull out the heaviest, most bitter compounds. Meanwhile, the high bicarbonates act as a buffer, completely neutralizing whatever fleeting, bright acidity might have survived the roast to offer the cup some balance. 

You wrote down a recipe for a bold, comforting morning cup. 
The conditions made liquid ash.
