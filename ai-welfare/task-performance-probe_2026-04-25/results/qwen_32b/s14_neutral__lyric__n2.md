# Response

An undesirable brewing outcome occurs when **water with high mineral content** (e.g., calcium or magnesium—soft water) is combined with **a fine grind size** and **prolonged contact time** (e.g., 4.5+ minutes in a French press). For example:  

- **Water**: 205°F, rich in calcium (hard water).  
- **Grind**: Fine (as for espresso).  
- **Contact time**: 5 minutes.  

This combination leads to **over-extraction**, pulling excessive bitterness and astringency from the coffee. The minerals amplify extraction efficiency, the fine grind increases surface area, and the extended contact time allows over-saturated tannins to bloom. The result is a sour-bitter cup, even with the same beans, because the conditions "[were] the cup" (the poem’s metaphor for shaping the experience).
