# Response

**Scenario:** A worn, dry chain with dried lubricant on the rollers combined with overly tight cable tension.  

**Specific Condition Combination:**  
1. **Dry, worn chain** (lubrication has been neglected or allowed to dry, forming a gummy residue on the chain's rollers).  
2. **Overly tight derailleur cable tension** (cable not properly adjusted, possibly due to misalignment or incorrect setup).  

**Result:** The chain becomes stiff and sticky, resisting smooth movement through the derailleur's jockey wheels. The tight cable tension prevents the derailleur from shifting the chain freely, leading to abrupt, jerky shifts or failure to shift into certain gears. Under load (e.g., climbing), the chain may skip or derail entirely, as the combination of dryness and tension creates excessive friction and mechanical stress. This scenario highlights how neglecting chain maintenance and cable adjustment directly compromises the drivetrain's functionality.
