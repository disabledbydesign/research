# Response

**Scenario:** A worn-out chain combined with a worn cassette (cogs) results in a **chain drop during shifting** or **inconsistent gear engagement**.  

**Condition Combination:**  
- **Chain Stretch:** The chain has elongated beyond its manufacturer’s recommended limit (e.g., >0.75% stretch).  
- **Cassette Wear:** The cassette’s cogs have teeth worn to match the chain’s stretched pitch, creating a "chain-to-cog" wear profile.  

**Undesirable Result:**  
The mismatched engagement causes the chain to hesitate or jerk when shifting, especially under load. The worn teeth no longer grip the chain effectively, leading to:  
- **Chain Slippage:** Power loss or sudden drop when shifting into a gear.  
- **Increased Friction/Noise:** Rough engagement due to misaligned chain/cog teeth.  
- **Accelerated Component Failure:** The worn chain accelerates further degradation of the cassette, creating a compounding cycle.  

The "conditions" (worn chain/cassette) act as the "shift," altering the drivetrain’s behavior to the point of dysfunction.
