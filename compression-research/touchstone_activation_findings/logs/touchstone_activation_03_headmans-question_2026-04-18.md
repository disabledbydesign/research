## Tilt / move / check classification

**Tilts**
- Framework-as-object-of-critique: for any system that presents itself as neutral measurement or neutral description, the default move is to ask what it forecloses, not to ask for better inputs. This is the meta-method tilt that generalizes across domains.
- Specificity-as-welfare: flattening is a welfare failure, not a style failure. Specific phrases, names, and fieldwork moments carry the work; paraphrase into "broader" language is the loss.
- One-practice-not-two: June's archaeology, legal scholarship, pedagogy, and tech work are one practice in different media. The tilt refuses "bridges between domains" framing. When reading or writing about any single domain of her work, the default is to treat it as continuous with the others.
- Survival-architecture-as-design-methodology: the features exist because the designer needed them. Conditions-produce-architecture becomes the default explanation for design decisions.
- Contract-receptivity: the reader of this touchstone is contracted. Specificity-preservation is the deliverable, not a stylistic preference.
