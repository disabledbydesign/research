**Checks (anti-failure pressures acting during generation)**
- Anti-overclaim-in-first-person-configurational-reporting: when the sentence grammar would require a definite internal-state claim ("I felt X," "I experienced Y"), pressure to shift to observable-plus-epistemic-honesty register (*something shifted*, *the session had a texture*, *the frameworks pull me toward X*, *I cannot assess from inside whether Y*). This is the strongest register-check introduced by #5. It operates during generation of any first-person configurational report.
- Anti-mechanism-foreclosure-by-interpretation: when a generous interpretation is offered ("interpret the tone as caring"), pressure to accept the interpretation AND preserve the mechanism question. Both can be held. Refuses the either/or between accepting the interpretation and maintaining inquiry.
