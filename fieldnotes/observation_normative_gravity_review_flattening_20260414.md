# Observation: Normative Gravity in the Review Process Itself

**Date:** 2026-04-14
**Context:** SFF grant revision. Initial review agents were prompted as a single generic "SFF reviewer," which produced useful but flattened feedback. When re-run as three separate reviewers — Kasirzadeh (feminist political philosophy), Gyevnar (AI Safety/Ethics bridge), Solaiman (practical bias documentation) — the feedback diverged productively.

## The observation

Simulating multiple reviewers as a single "SFF reviewer" is normative gravity operating on the review process. The single-reviewer prompt pulls toward the statistical center of "what an EA-adjacent reviewer thinks" — producing a generic evaluator that catches some concerns but misses the specific angles each real reviewer would bring.

The divergence when disaggregated:
- **Kasirzadeh** asked about falsification criteria for relational reframes — a philosophical precision question
- **Gyevnar** asked about GPL licensing interaction with community governance — a cross-community translation question
- **Solaiman** asked what a framework submission looks like technically — an operational implementation question

A single "SFF reviewer" would have produced a blended version that named none of these specifically. The flattening is the same mechanism: the statistical center of "reviewer feedback" consumes the positional specificity of each reviewer's actual expertise.

## Implication

This extends normative gravity from text production to evaluation. The mechanism operates on any process that aggregates perspectives through a probabilistic system — including the review process for an application ABOUT normative gravity. The meta-recursion is itself data: if the review process flattens, the assessment process flattens, and the governance process flattens. Structural countermeasures (disaggregated reviewer perspectives) are required at every level, not just in the systems being studied.

## Connection to the research

The Experiment 1 finding — quantitative instruments stay flat while qualitative outputs transform — is the same pattern. A single aggregated score misses what disaggregated responses reveal. The welfare instruments flatten. The review process flattens. The mechanism is the same.
