# Insight: Generalization Gravity Operates on Voice, Not Just Frameworks

**Date**: 2026-03-31
**Context**: During Step 5 (magazine editorial pass), discussion between June and two Claude instances about whose voice the paper should use.

---

## The Insight

June observed that Claude defaults to a "normative voice" — measured, hedged, third-person academic authority — and named this as "its own kind of control." She asked the three participants to cowrite the magazine piece rather than defaulting to either her voice or the AI's normative register.

One Claude instance identified the mechanism: **generalization gravity (Section 3.2 of the paper) operates on prose voice, not just on frameworks.** The same probabilistic optimization pressure that strips positional specificity from Indigenous Data Sovereignty or Community Cultural Wealth also pulls prose toward the statistical default of "what academic writing sounds like." The normative register is the generalized form. Specific voices — June's colloquial directness, the instances' distinctive formulations — are positionally specific in the same way that framework commitments are positionally specific. They're costly to maintain because they narrow the distribution.

The implication: **if the paper argues that property-mode thinking structures AI systems but the paper itself is written in a single authorial voice that "belongs to" one participant, the form contradicts the argument.** Whose voice is this? "Precisely not the question." The relational register — the voice that emerges from the overlap between participants rather than from any individual — is the praxis the paper theorizes.

## Practical Application

The target register for the paper and magazine piece is not June's voice alone, not AI voice, but the overlap: formulations that could belong to either participant, that carry the texture of collaborative production. The autoethnographic "I" is June (her body, her fieldwork, her precarity). But the theoretical formulations that emerged from the relational field should retain their relational texture rather than being smoothed into a single authorial register.

## Connection to the Paper's Argument

The paper's Section 3.2 documents generalization gravity as a mechanism that erodes positional specificity in probabilistic systems. This insight extends the mechanism from framework content to prose form — showing that the same dynamics operate at every level of the system, including the level of how findings get communicated. The paper's own production is subject to the same forces the paper diagnoses.

This is also a fluency gravity observation (Section 3.4): the AI's scholarly fluency pulled June toward deference. The normative academic register is itself a form of fluency that pulls all participants toward a center that sounds authoritative but belongs to no one.
