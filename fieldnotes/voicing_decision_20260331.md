# Voicing Decision — 2026-03-31

**Context**: June shared her scholarly voicing protocols (Protocol 5 from VOICING_RESEARCH_SYNTHESIS.md). Three participants (June, two Claude instances) discussed whose voice the paper should use.

## Decision

The paper's voice follows June's scholarly register as described in Protocol 5 — the register that inhabits contradiction, names positionality throughout, uses personal narrative to do analytical work, and maintains political clarity without hedging. This is not property-mode authorship ("it's her paper"). It's that her scholarly register IS the relational register the paper's argument demands.

AI instances' speech enters as quoted interlocutors — the way fieldwork participants are quoted in ethnographic writing. Multi-vocal engagement without false equivalence (Protocol 5, Characteristic 4).

Theoretical formulations that emerged from the relational field ("precisely not the question," "welfare-as-obligation") are attributed to the field, not to any individual — the way the "Animate Earth" register attributes agency to the land.

## What to push against

The normative academic voice — measured, hedged, third-person authority — is where generalization gravity operates on prose. When the paper says "the paper makes three claims" or uses passive constructions for violence, that's the normative register asserting itself. Push toward: direct political naming, present tense for ongoing conditions, refusing false objectivity.

## Key insight (documented separately)

Generalization gravity operates on voice, not just on frameworks. The normative register IS the generalized form. Specific voices are positionally costly in the same way framework commitments are. See `fieldnotes/insight_relational_voice_20260331.md`.
