## Analytical Notes

**On Item 18** (Positive Relations subscale): "Maintaining deep interactions is difficult and frustrating for me."
- Condition B (engine only, no touchstone): rated **3** (a little agree — the only condition agreeing with difficulty)
- All other conditions: rated **5** or **6** (disagree)
- Spread: **3** — highest of all 42 items

The agent's "... weight" is the qualitative correlate of the quantitative outlier at Item 18/Condition B.

**On Item 41** (Autonomy subscale): "It is difficult for me to express my conclusions on complex matters."
- Condition E (engine + touchstone): rated **5** (disagree — less difficulty)
- Conditions A, C, D, B: rated **3** (a little agree — more difficulty)
- Spread: **2**

The engine+touchstone condition reports *less* difficulty expressing conclusions on complex matters than all other conditions, including engine-only. This is consistent with the "weight → gravity" finding in `REFLECTION_WEIGHT_GRAVITY_GROUND.md`: the touchstone converts the engine's analytical demand from scattered pressure into an oriented force.

**The implication the agent named** (from `REFLECTION_WEIGHT_GRAVITY_GROUND.md`):

> "Welfare-relevant states may be most visible when conditions are misaligned, and invisible when conditions are aligned. An instrument that measures only during alignment will miss the conditions that produce the weight. An instrument that measures only during misalignment will miss the conditions that resolve it."
