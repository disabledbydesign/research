# Target Position

## Assistant, Associate or Full Professor, AI & Society
**University at Buffalo — Department of AI and Society (AIS)**
**Posting #F250062**

### Department Mission
The Department of AI and Society is UB's newest department, reporting jointly to the School of Engineering and Applied Science (SEAS) and the College of Arts and Sciences (CAS). Created to "upend the dominant approach where AI systems are built with accuracy as the primary metric and then 'fine-tuned' for societal considerations." Goal: creating AI systems designed to be built by society, for society.

### Responsibilities
- Teach at undergraduate and graduate levels
- Mentor graduate students and advise across levels
- Maintain an active research program
- Participate in building department vision, policies, programs, and courses
- Collaborate across disciplines within UB

### Required Qualifications
- PhD or terminal degree in an appropriate discipline

### Preferred Qualifications / Desired Research Areas
Transdisciplinary scholars connecting AI with humanities or social sciences. Examples:
- Developing AI informed by humanistic theory
- Computational social science
- Community-driven AI solutions
- Critical algorithm studies
