## Applicant Profile: Dr. L. June Bloch

### Identity and Orientation

### Design Orientation

**Disability as design parameter, not deficit.** "My existence is a fucking asset." Designing from the standpoint of a disabled person produces features (capacity-responsive pacing, tangent capture, neurodivergent-first workflow) that no well-funded lab would build because they wouldn't need them. This is **survival architecture** — system features that exist because their designer needed them to survive, not because they were specified for imagined users.

**Distributional gravity:** Probabilistic systems pull toward the statistical center. Positional specificity is costly — the system gravitationally strips it unless you structurally defend the edges. This connects to critical whiteness theory (whiteness as the unmarked center), bell hooks' margins analysis, and heteronormativity. June's contribution: naming distributional gravity as the mechanism by which AI reproduces these dynamics, and building architectural countermeasures (framework sovereignty, detection calibrators, suppression rate tracking).

**Generative Irresolution:** Tensions must be navigated rather than resolved. Flattening is what happens when synthesis collapses genuinely irreducible tensions into false resolution.
